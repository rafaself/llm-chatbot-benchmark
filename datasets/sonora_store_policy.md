# Sonora — Customer Service, Purchasing, and Customer Relations Policies

Sonora sells musical instruments, accessories, audio equipment, and related products through its online store and physical location in Lyravale, in the province of Bellwether, within the fictional realm of Elaria. We are committed to providing clear information, honoring the terms offered at the time of purchase, advising customers on the proper use of products, and offering transparent solutions for questions, orders, deliveries, and post-sale issues.

The following policies form part of the terms governing our relationship with consumers. They apply to purchases made directly through our official channels, without prejudice to consumer protection rules and rights established under the Elarian Consumer Fairness Code (ECFC). If an internal commercial rule is less favorable than a right guaranteed by the ECFC, that right prevails. Special terms of a valid offer that are more favorable to the consumer will be honored within the limits of that offer.

## 1. General Rules and Interpretation of the Policies

### 1.1. Scope and Sales Channels

Sonora serves individuals and businesses, including musicians, students, teachers, music schools, studios, and audio professionals. Sales take place through the online store, authorized sales representatives, and the physical store. The contracting method, place of purchase, terms expressly advertised, and nature of the product may determine different procedures.

Purchases made online or through another means outside the commercial establishment are subject to e-commerce rules, including the statutory right of withdrawal where applicable. A purchase completed in person at the physical store does not automatically carry the same right of withdrawal. In that case, in addition to statutory warranties, customers may use the preference exchange options offered under this policy or the specific offer.

For products sold by third parties on external platforms, the information provided for the relevant sale governs communication channels and platform-specific matters. Sonora remains responsible for its legal obligations as a supplier when it is identified as the seller on the order. Purchases made directly from another seller may not appear in our systems and do not authorize access to that seller's data.

### 1.2. Order of Precedence

The following order applies when interpreting a situation: (a) rights and obligations established by law; (b) express terms of the offer accepted by the consumer, including more favorable special terms; (c) category- or service-specific terms provided before contracting; (d) the general rules in this document; and (e) operational customer service guidance. No advertisement, coupon, benefit-program rules, or representative's guidance may limit statutory rights.

The information recorded for an individual order confirmation determines the products, quantities, prices actually agreed upon, delivery method, and estimated date shown at checkout. General information on the Sonora portal does not replace individual terms already accepted. Customer service must review any discrepancies without making a unilateral change that is detrimental to the customer.

### 1.3. Definitions

- **Calendar day:** Any day of the calendar, including Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays.
- **Operational business day:** Monday through Friday, excluding national holidays and provincial or municipal holidays observed by the unit responsible for the operation. For central processing, the Lyravale calendar applies.
- **Meridian Time:** The reference time zone for times, due dates, and electronic records, unless another time zone is expressly specified at contracting.
- **Confirmed order:** An order for which payment has been recognized and commercial verification completed, or an order placed using an authorized method with subsequent billing.
- **In-stock product:** An item recorded as available for picking from the inventory allocated to that sales method.
- **Special order:** An item purchased subject to a specific estimate for procurement, manufacture, import, or preparation, provided before purchase.
- **Customized product:** An item adapted at an individual's request, such as through engraving, a special finish, or a non-standard configuration.
- **Product defect:** A problem with quality, quantity, or suitability that affects conformity with the offer or expected use, assessed in light of the Elarian Consumer Fairness Code.
- **Preference exchange:** A voluntary exchange of a non-defective product for reasons of taste, color, model, or size, under the commercial terms of this policy.
- **Withdrawal:** Statutory withdrawal from contracts entered into outside the commercial establishment, as applicable.
- **Case number:** An identifier provided to track a registered request.

### 1.4. Deadlines and Records

Deadlines stated in calendar days include weekends and holidays; deadlines stated in business days exclude non-operational days as defined in Section 1.3. Requests submitted through the online portal are received 24 hours a day and retain their submission time, including on weekends and holidays. The end of human customer service hours does not prevent the exercise of a right within the statutory period.

For commercial customer service and operational deadlines stated in business days, an order or request received after 5:59 p.m. will be forwarded to the operational queue on the next business day, without changing its registration date. A statutory deadline applicable to the consumer is not shortened or extended by this internal convention. If a local holiday affects only a carrier or the destination location, any change to the estimated delivery date will be communicated as described in Section 7.

### 1.5. Good Faith and Cooperation

The company and its representatives must provide accurate information, handle requests respectfully, avoid abusive practices, and seek solutions proportionate to the facts. Customers must provide accurate information needed for delivery and contact, retain available proof of purchase, and report relevant circumstances as precisely as possible. No unnecessary documentation requirement will be imposed to prevent the exercise of statutory rights.

## 2. Store Operations, Locations, and Official Channels

### 2.1. Physical Store

The Lyravale location is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and Saturdays from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., Meridian Time. There is no regular in-person service on Sundays. Addresses and information about access and parking are provided in the **Stores and Pickup** section in the Sonora portal; the location selected on the order confirms the applicable pickup point.

The physical store offers equipment demonstrations subject to availability, usage guidance, sales, pickup of authorized orders, and receipt of post-sale requests. Technical diagnostics and repairs are performed by qualified staff or service providers by appointment; immediate completion during a visit is not guaranteed.

### 2.2. Remote Customer Service

Live customer service through the **Help Center** chat, the **Contact Us** form, and the Sonora Message Line listed in the store portal is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and Saturdays from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. Digital channels for checking orders, submitting requests and documents, and exercising the right of withdrawal are available at all times, except during technical outages addressed in Section 16.

Customers do not need to wait for live customer service to file a complaint, request cancellation, or seek urgent action. The electronic receipt provides the case number and registration time. Sonora will not request passwords, one-time codes, full card numbers, or confidential banking information in messages.

### 2.3. Holidays, Special Dates, and Exceptional Operations

The physical store is closed on national holidays and on provincial or municipal holidays observed in Lyravale, except when special hours are expressly announced in the **Stores and Pickup** section. Remote live customer service may operate on a reduced schedule on special dates, as announced in the store portal. The shipping facility operates Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., except for holidays observed at the distribution center.

During periods of high demand, such as seasonal campaigns, the company may extend hours and add staff without changing rights already secured by the customer. Extended physical-store hours do not mean that the carrier will collect or deliver on the same day. Processing times and delivery commitments are those provided at checkout.

### 2.4. Accessible Customer Service

Customers may indicate a preference for written service and request simplified explanations of technical or financial terms. Essential information about prices, delivery, product condition, warranty, and cancellation will be provided in a legible format that does not rely exclusively on verbal communication. Accessibility requests are referred to the responsible team for a solution consistent with available resources and legal obligations.

### 2.5. Channel Identification

The company's registration and contact information, including supplier identification and service address, is available in the **About Us** section, the Sonora portal, and tax documents when issued. Official communications regarding order changes, return authorizations, and payment information take place in the authenticated order environment or through contacts listed in those channels.

## 3. Customer Relations and Resolution of Requests

### 3.1. Types of Requests

The Help Center accepts pre-purchase questions, compatibility inquiries, payment issues, inventory discrepancies, delivery requests, exchanges, withdrawals, product defects, warranty and technical service requests, privacy matters, and complaints about customer service.

Each request receives an individual case number or is linked to an existing one when it concerns the same matter. Customers can check its status without having to repeat the entire account, subject to identity and security checks when necessary.

### 3.2. Acknowledgment and First Response Times

Receipt of electronically submitted requests is acknowledged automatically and immediately. As an internal target, the first human response is sent within **1 operational business day**. A response to information requests, questions, complaints, or requests to suspend or cancel a contract made through e-commerce will be sent within **5 days**, without prejudice to shorter statutory deadlines or actions that must be taken immediately. When resolution depends on an expert assessment, carrier, financial institution, or technical supplier, the response will state the steps taken and the next follow-up milestone; it will not be treated as a final resolution while the matter remains pending.

Urgent requests, such as suspected fraud, delivery to an incorrect address, electrical hazards, or product damage that poses a danger, are prioritized for containment, without a promise of an immediate response outside live customer service hours.

### 3.3. Required Information and Identity Verification

To locate an order, the customer may be asked for the order number and a confirmation detail previously associated with the account. Access to a full address, payment information, order history, or another person's personal data requires verification proportionate to the risk. Customer service will not disclose another customer's data based solely on knowledge of the order number.

Privacy requests are handled under Section 13. No identification or personal document is required for general questions about policies, public prices, or compatibility.

### 3.4. Handling Complaints

Complaints should describe the facts, include the order number when available, and state the desired outcome. Photographs of the packaging, labels, item, and defect may help with the investigation but are not a universal requirement to open a case. The company may request additional material that is necessary and proportionate to the situation, explaining its purpose.

The review considers offer documents, available proof, internal records, the consumer's account, and information from other parties involved. Conclusions will be communicated objectively, with reasons and applicable alternatives. Customers may request a review of the response through the **Review Customer Service** channel, which will assign the case to a different analyst whenever operationally possible.

### 3.5. Escalation of Disputes

If the first step does not resolve the matter, customer service may refer it to commercial supervision, the logistics team, technical service, or the person responsible for data protection. Escalation does not suspend statutory deadlines. At any time, consumers may contact the Elarian Consumer Resolution Office, use the Elaria Resolve service when the company is enrolled, contact other competent authorities, or bring a claim before the courts; no prior attempt to reach a private settlement is required.

### 3.6. Conduct Rules

Representatives must not promise inventory, timelines, or discounts that are not authorized and documented. A statutory right may not be conditioned on accepting store credit, signing a general release, posting a positive review, or waiving another right. Customers and representatives must communicate respectfully. Threatening situations may be referred to the appropriate team, while preserving access to appropriate service channels and the handling of legitimate requests.

## 4. Catalog, Categories, and Product Information

### 4.1. Product Categories

The catalog includes string instruments (acoustic guitars, electric guitars, basses, ukuleles, and bowed instruments), keyboards and digital pianos, drums and percussion, wind instruments, microphones, audio interfaces, mixing consoles, studio monitors, speakers, amplifiers, pedals and processors, cables, connectors, stands, cases, strings, drumsticks, picks, power supplies, and related accessories.

Products may be new, display models, pre-owned, or refurbished. Their condition will be disclosed before purchase, along with any signs of use, replaced parts, missing accessories, and functional tests. A reduced price for a display model does not eliminate the statutory warranty for undisclosed defects or relieve the company of responsibility for an inaccurate offer.

### 4.2. Information Included in the Offer

Where relevant, each item's description states its brand, model, color, voltage, connections, dimensions, weight, package contents, compatibility, power requirements, included accessories, finish type, condition, availability policy, and any special timelines. Illustrative images do not prevail over express specifications, but images and advertising messages must accurately represent the product actually offered.

Consumers should check voltage, connectors, impedance, power, and compatibility with other equipment. If the company expressly states specific compatibility in an advertisement or formal pre-sale guidance, that information forms part of the offer and will be considered in any assessment of product suitability.

### 4.3. Natural Variations and Specific Characteristics

Wooden instruments may have natural variations in grain, tone, and finish within the advertised specification. Minor differences inherent to the material do not, by themselves, constitute a defect; however, substantial differences from the model, promised color, or description will be treated as a discrepancy from the offer.

Instrument tuning, initial setup, and stability depend on environmental factors and handling. This does not relieve the company of its obligation to deliver a product suitable for normal use. Special adjustments requested by the customer must be included in the order or in a separate service order.

### 4.4. Electrical and Audio Products

Customers should observe the rated voltage, plug type, grounding requirements, power rating, and ventilation conditions. Products that support multiple voltages are expressly identified as such; if no such indication is provided, automatic voltage compatibility must not be assumed. Imported equipment will be supplied with usage instructions and other information required for sale in Elaria.

Studio monitors and speakers may be sold individually or in pairs, as stated in the description. The prices and quantities in the cart prevail over assumptions about kits. Pedals may be sold without a power supply when this is clearly indicated. Cables, adapters, and interfaces should be selected according to the specified connections.

### 4.5. Digital Products and Related Services

When activation codes, software licenses, and digital content are sold, their technical requirements, delivery method, activation limits, license duration, and the software owner's terms will be provided before purchase. No advertised commercial restriction on digital content automatically waives the right of withdrawal or other statutory rights applicable to distance contracts.

Installation, custom setup, and maintenance services are included in a purchase only when itemized. Shipping an instrument does not, by default, include an in-home technical visit, complex assembly, or electrical installation. Separately contracted services are subject to the estimate and post-sale rules in Section 11.

## 5. Inventory, Reservations, Special Orders, and Availability

### 5.1. Displayed Availability

The labels **Available**, **Limited stock**, **Special order**, and **Unavailable** describe the status shown at the time of display. Availability may vary between the physical store, distribution center, and suppliers. Adding an item to the cart does not constitute a confirmed reservation; final availability is checked when the order is processed.

The company uses inventory controls to reduce discrepancies and promptly communicates situations that prevent fulfillment of an offer. Unavailability identified after contracting does not authorize the supplier to substitute another product without consent, impose store credit, or remove the consumer's legal alternatives.

### 5.2. Products Available for Shipment

For in-stock items with confirmed payment and no additional verification, picking begins in the next operational cycle. The estimated ship date appears on the order and is normally within **2 operational business days**, unless a different period is expressly shown at contracting. Fragile, oversized, or specially prepared items may require up to **4 operational business days** for picking, provided this condition was disclosed before confirmation.

The label **in stock for immediate shipment** does not mean same-day delivery. The arrival date depends on preparation, shipping method, destination postal code, and logistics events described in Section 7.

### 5.3. Reservation for In-Store Purchase

When offered by the physical store, a reservation without payment lasts **24 consecutive hours** from written confirmation and is subject to the terms provided when the reservation is made. It expires automatically at the end of that period; holidays or store closures do not extend it, unless the confirmation itself specifies the first pickup opportunity after reopening.

The **In-store pickup** option on an already-paid order is not an unpaid reservation. It is subject to the specific availability and holding periods in Section 7.7.

### 5.4. Special Orders and Pre-orders

Special-order or pre-order items must display an estimated lead time or availability date, the source of the estimate, and the terms of supply. If the estimate depends on manufacturing or import, the consumer will be informed before contracting. The order's timeline will be recorded and may not be changed unilaterally to the customer's detriment.

Significant supply delays will be communicated together with the legal alternatives: fulfillment of the offer, an equivalent product if accepted by the customer, or termination with any applicable refund. A product's customized or special-order status does not automatically eliminate withdrawal rights for contracts entered into outside the commercial establishment or rights relating to defects or failure to fulfill the offer.

### 5.5. Orders with Multiple Items

Shipping items together is standard when all items share the same method and logistics origin. If an order contains both an in-stock item and a special-order item, the estimate shown at checkout must state whether shipment will be combined or split. A subsequent split shipment may occur only at no additional cost to the consumer and while preserving the agreed delivery terms, or by express agreement.

When a partial shipment is authorized, shipped and pending items will remain separately identified for tracking, cancellation, and any return. The consumer is not required to accept a material change to the timeline for pending items without an appropriate solution.

### 5.6. Discontinuation, Unexpected Unavailability, and Substitution

If a manufacturer discontinues production or an inventory synchronization error occurs, Sonora will check actual options for fulfilling the offer and inform the customer of appropriate alternatives. A similar product will be shipped only with the customer's explicit consent. A favorable price difference or a change in brand or color does not replace that consent.

If the offer cannot be fulfilled and the consumer chooses to terminate the contract, amounts received will be refunded under the applicable legal procedures, without deductions for an inventory failure. Any damages recognized under applicable rules are not limited by this policy.

## 6. Prices, Payments, and Invoicing

### 6.1. Currency, Price Components, and Transparency

Prices are stated in dollars. The displayed product price includes applicable taxes incorporated into the final offered price, without prejudice to tax itemization. Shipping, optional services, and other permitted charges are clearly disclosed before order confirmation. Optional services are not added automatically without authorization.

The offer states any terms tied to the payment method, sales channel, or availability. Prices may vary across campaigns and channels, provided this is clearly disclosed and does not adversely affect orders already placed.

### 6.2. Sonora Transfer Instant Payments

Eligible orders may be paid through Sonora Transfer, Elaria's instant payment network, using a code or key displayed exclusively in the Sonora payment environment. The QR code for a standard order remains available for **30 consecutive minutes**, unless a different validity period is shown before completion. If payment is not identified within that period, inventory may be released and the attempted purchase closed, subject to the ECFC and the handling of payments actually made.

Payments confirmed after the code expires will be reviewed. If the amount cannot be matched to a valid order, the company will arrange a refund to the legitimate payer through the appropriate mechanisms. Customers are not required to make another payment in response to a message from an unofficial channel.

### 6.3. Card Payments

Cards from the brands and issuers shown at checkout are accepted. A transaction may be subject to authorization, fraud prevention, and verification by the issuer. Card authorization alone does not constitute final order confirmation; the consumer will be notified of any rejection, pending status, or need for verification.

When available, interest-free installment payments allow up to **6 installments**, with a minimum amount of **$70.00 per installment**. The number of installments offered at checkout depends on the eligible total and transaction terms. Any interest-bearing option, if offered, will be shown before confirmation, including the total cost and terms required by law.

### 6.4. Bank Payment Slips

When available, a bank payment slip is due **2 operational business days** after issuance, unless a specific due date appears on the document. Bank settlement may take up to **2 business days** after payment. The order is not released for shipment until payment is recognized. If no valid payment is made, the associated reservation may be closed after settlement is checked.

Customers should verify the payee, amount, and billing details through official channels. The company does not replace payment slips with attachments received in unsolicited messages.

### 6.5. Payment Method Discount

Sonora may offer a **3% discount on the subtotal of eligible products paid in full via Sonora Transfer** when the benefit is active and visible at checkout. The discount does not apply to shipping, services, gift cards, or products already subject to a specific promotional discount; it cannot be combined with a fixed-value coupon, points redemption, or other exclusive pricing terms unless the promotion expressly allows it. Regular-priced products in the same cart may receive the discount even when other items are on sale, if this rule is itemized in the final summary.

No discount will be applied retroactively to an order already confirmed unless the campaign provides for it. The amount relevant to refunds and benefit calculations is the amount actually paid, subject to statutory rights.

### 6.6. Price Discrepancies and Listing Errors

Discrepancies between a price tag, product page, cart, and confirmation must be reviewed based on the offer actually presented and consumer protection rules. The company will not unilaterally change the price of an order already placed or assume that every system error permits it to reject an offer. Obvious material errors will be reviewed individually, taking into account the clarity of the information, the parties' good faith, the transaction history, and applicable law.

If a discrepancy is noticed before the purchase is completed, the customer may request clarification without any obligation to buy. If it arises after contracting, the company will explain the basis for its assessment and the legally available solutions, without imposing an unaccepted additional payment.

### 6.7. Tax Invoices and Proof of Purchase

The applicable tax documentation will be issued in accordance with tax law and made available with the order or sent through the registered channels. Temporary inability to access a tax document does not eliminate the warranty or the right to file a claim; other reliable evidence may establish the consumer transaction.

Customers must provide accurate information for tax invoicing. Subsequent corrections will be assessed under tax rules, without promising changes prohibited after issuance.

### 6.8. Fraud Review and Payment Protection

Transactions may undergo automated and, when necessary, human review to prevent fraud. Such review must be proportionate and respect necessity, security, and personal data protection. Consumers will be advised of any pending issues through an official channel. Requests for excessive proof or sensitive card data are prohibited.

If the review prevents the transaction from proceeding, Sonora will notify the customer and facilitate appropriate procedures for refunding recognized payments. Rights relating to automated decisions and information about data processing are addressed in Section 13.
## 7. Orders, Shipping, Dispatch, and Delivery

### 7.1. Processing Stages

An order may have the following statuses: **Awaiting payment**, **Payment under review**, **Confirmed**, **Being picked**, **Ready for pickup**, **Shipped**, **Delivered**, **Cancellation requested**, **Canceled**, **Return in progress**, and **Refunded**. These statuses indicate operational progress; they do not replace proof of delivery or change consumer rights.

Dispatch takes place after payment is confirmed and the required checks are completed, in accordance with the estimate recorded at checkout. Customers will be contacted about outstanding address or essential documentation issues; the company does not assume authorization to change the recipient, delivery location, or shipping method.

### 7.2. Service Areas

The online store serves addresses in Elaria where logistics coverage is confirmed at checkout. Availability depends on the postal code, the product's dimensions and characteristics, and applicable shipping rules. Rural locations, islands, areas with traffic restrictions, addresses without regular access, and regions subject to special conditions may require a specific method, pickup at a designated location, or may make direct delivery unavailable.

If checkout does not offer a shipping method for a particular postal code, customers may ask customer service about alternatives before placing an order. The company does not advertise unrestricted nationwide coverage for categories requiring special transportation.

### 7.3. Shipping Costs and Delivery Time Calculation

Shipping is calculated based on the postal code, distance, actual or dimensional weight, dimensions, number of packages, declared value where applicable, carrier availability, and selected method. The order summary shows the cost and timeline before purchase confirmation. Charges for an authorized optional service are itemized separately.

The timeline shown at checkout includes preparation and transit, according to the offered method. If the system shows **processing time** and **transit time** separately, the customer will be told that the estimated arrival is the operational sum of those stages, calculated from the stated starting point. Transit time alone will not be used as a final delivery promise.

Under normal conditions, preparation time starts on the first business day after payment confirmation, and transit time begins when the carrier records physical receipt of the goods. The delivery commitment shown at contracting prevails over these general rules whenever it is more specific or favorable to the consumer.

### 7.4. Shipping Methods

- **Economy delivery:** Transportation with an estimate provided by postal code and no specific time appointment.
- **Express delivery:** An option available only where coverage exists, with its own price and estimate.
- **Special freight:** A method for bulky, fragile, or heavy instruments or equipment, with appropriate packaging and handling; any need for an appointment will be disclosed.
- **In-store pickup:** Delivery to the customer or an authorized representative after notice that the order is available, as described in Section 7.7.

No method promises delivery at an exact time unless the order expressly commits to it. The company may not replace a paid method with a materially inferior one without appropriately addressing the difference and the agreed terms.

### 7.5. Free Shipping

The standard **free economy shipping** offer, when displayed at checkout, applies to orders with a subtotal of **$599.00 or more**, after product discounts, coupons, and points and before shipping is added. The benefit depends on coverage for the postal code and excludes items explicitly marked **Special Freight** on the product page. Those items remain available with the shipping cost disclosed before payment.

Free shipping does not automatically apply to express delivery or scheduled methods. A campaign may offer more favorable terms by identifying the products, regions, minimum amount, and method. If the benefit does not appear at checkout, this must be clarified before contracting; shipping terms granted and recorded may not be withdrawn from an order already confirmed.

### 7.6. Tracking and Logistics Updates

After dispatch, the order displays a tracking code or link when provided by the carrier. Updates may lag behind the physical movement of the shipment due to technical delays. If a shipment in transit has no update for **3 operational business days**, a logistics investigation may be opened; customers do not have to wait for the delivery deadline to report a suspected loss.

Customer service will report the outcome of the investigation and relevant actions. The investigation period does not automatically suspend the consumer's rights in the event of failure to fulfill the offer or non-delivery.

### 7.7. In-Store Pickup

Customers should not come to collect a paid order until its status is **Ready for pickup**. When the items are available at the location, ordinary preparation takes up to **1 operational business day** after payment confirmation; internal transfers or products from other locations will have a specific estimate shown at checkout.

The order remains available for pickup for **7 consecutive days** after notice. If the customer does not come, the team will make contact to arrange another date, delivery for a shipping charge, or another solution. The amount paid will not be automatically forfeited or retained in full because the customer did not collect the order. The consumer may request cancellation according to the contracting method and Section 12.

To collect an order, the account holder must present appropriate identification and the order number. Pickup by a third party requires authorization previously recorded on the order and identity verification proportionate to the risk. Unnecessary copies of documents will not be required for simple procedures.

### 7.8. Delivery Address and Changes

Customers must provide a complete and correct address, including postal code, number, unit or other address details, and access instructions where relevant. Before dispatch, changes may be requested through the **Change delivery** channel. The change will be assessed for feasibility, price, fraud prevention, and arrival date, with express confirmation required before any additional charge.

After dispatch, address changes depend on the carrier and may require a new quote and timeline. The company does not promise redirection without operational confirmation. If an address error results from incorrect information provided by the customer and causes a return or reshipment, new and verifiable costs may be presented before the new shipment is made, subject to statutory rights. Registration or dispatch errors attributable to Sonora will be corrected at no cost to the customer.

### 7.9. Delivery Attempts and Recipient Absence

The carrier will make the attempts provided for under the contracted method, generally up to **2 attempts** when access and operational conditions permit. The attempt policy does not guarantee carrier access to gated communities with restrictions, incomplete addresses, or unsafe locations.

If delivery fails because the recipient is absent or access is blocked, the customer will receive guidance on rescheduling, pickup at an authorized location, or return to sender, depending on the method. Deliveries not completed due to carrier failure do not result in additional shipping charges to the consumer. A reshipping charge may be considered only when it results from a cause attributable to the consumer, is disclosed in advance, and is legally permissible.

### 7.10. Inspection Upon Receipt

We recommend checking the recipient's name, the number of packages, and any signs of tampering, severe crushing, moisture, or impact. If there is visible damage, the customer may note it, photograph the package, and refuse delivery. Accepting a delivery without noting an issue does not eliminate rights relating to defects, concealed damage, or discrepancies discovered later.

If the wrong item is delivered, an accessory is missing, the package has been tampered with, or shipping damage is found, the consumer may contact the Help Center as soon as the issue is noticed. Photos, labels, and receipts may help, but the company will not automatically reject a complaint because there is no photograph taken at the door or no witness.

### 7.11. Delays, Loss, Theft, and Misdelivery

If there is evidence of a significant delay, loss, or misdelivery, Sonora will investigate with the carrier and keep the consumer informed. If the delivery obligation is not fulfilled, the consumer will have the legal alternatives of requiring fulfillment of the offer, choosing to accept an equivalent product, or terminating the purchase with the applicable refund, without prejudice to other rights.

The company does not transfer to the consumer, by a mere commercial clause, the ordinary risk of transportation it arranged to deliver the product. Telling the customer to resolve the issue alone with the carrier does not replace the store's responsibility in the consumer relationship.

### 7.12. Split Deliveries and Oversized Products

When an order is shipped in multiple packages, each package may have its own tracking. Delivery of one package does not authorize marking the order as fully delivered if another required package is still pending.

Digital pianos with stands, drum kits, heavy amplifiers, and stage equipment may require two-person transportation, a suitable elevator, or specific access. Known restrictions must be disclosed in the offer or when scheduling. Standard delivery does not include hoisting, door removal, hoisting through a facade, or special installation unless expressly contracted. If the promised method is not suitable for the item, the company will make the necessary correction without imposing an uncontracted cost on the consumer.

## 8. Promotions, Coupons, and Commercial Campaigns

### 8.1. Required Campaign Information

Each campaign will identify participating products, start and end dates, applicable channel, benefit offered, any limits per Elarian customer number or order, geographic or inventory restrictions, eligible payment methods, and stacking conditions. Advertising must be clear and sufficient to support an informed decision.

Validity periods stated in hours refer to Meridian Time. If a campaign ends on a date without a specified time, it ends at **11:59:59 p.m.** on that date, unless a more favorable rule or express specification applies. The time relevant to eligibility is the order confirmation time at checkout, not the delivery time.

### 8.2. Promotional Prices

A promotional price applies to the items, quantities, and terms of the campaign. Products already purchased are not subject to a retroactive reduction merely because their price later falls; likewise, subsequent price increases do not change confirmed orders. Statements of prior prices and discount percentages must reflect the terms actually advertised and must not use misleading comparisons.

**Last units** or **limited stock** promotions must reflect actual availability and respect the rights of customers who have already contracted. A generic claim that an item is sold out does not eliminate the legal alternatives available in the event of failure to fulfill an offer.

### 8.3. Discount Coupons

Coupons may be percentage-based, fixed-value, or shipping-related. As a general rule, **1 coupon per order** is allowed. A fixed-value coupon is limited to the subtotal of eligible products and never generates a cash balance. Percentage coupons apply only to participating products, after automatic price discounts already applied, when the campaign allows that combination.

By default, the one product discount coupon allowed on an order cannot be combined with promotional discounts on the same item, the Sonora Transfer discount, or points redemption. A campaign may allow that coupon to be combined with those other benefits. The exception must be stated in the offer details and in the payment summary. It does not allow a second coupon on the same order. The system will show the effective result before purchase completion; consumers do not need to infer the order in which discounts apply.

### 8.4. Shipping Benefits and Restrictions

A free-shipping coupon applies only to the method and postal codes specified in the campaign. It does not make special freight free when the campaign excludes that category. If the offer sets a minimum based on the subtotal, the calculation uses eligible products after discounts and excludes shipping and services.

The free-shipping terms in Section 7.5 may be combined with discounts on product prices because the minimum threshold already uses the net subtotal. No cash payment is made for any unused portion of a shipping benefit.

### 8.5. Individual Limits and Abuse Prevention

Campaigns may set a maximum number of units per consumer, provided the limit is disclosed before purchase and does not constitute improper discrimination. ## 9. Exchanges, Returns, and the Right of Withdrawal

No voluntary exchange policy may restrict a legally available return, refund, replacement, or repair.

For contracts entered into outside the commercial establishment, including online or by telephone, the consumer may exercise the statutory right of withdrawal within **7 consecutive days**, counted from signing the contract or receiving the product or service, as applicable. For physical goods sold at a distance, Sonora uses the date the consumer receives the relevant product as the practical starting point. For separate items delivered in installments, the period for each item starts upon its own delivery; for an indivisible set, the complete delivery is considered.

Customers may request withdrawal through the same tool used to make the purchase, under **Orders > Request a return**, or through any other available official channel. Receipt of the request will be acknowledged immediately. A timely exercise of this right does not require justification, discretionary approval, a technical report, or prior authorization to be valid.

### 9.3. Costs and Refunds Upon Withdrawal

A valid withdrawal does not incur a penalty or return shipping cost for the consumer. The company will provide reverse logistics or equivalent instructions at no charge, taking dimensions and location into account. For a full withdrawal, the amounts paid for the purchase, including the original shipping charge, will be refunded subject to the applicable legal refund rules and payment methods.

When applicable, notice of withdrawal will be sent immediately to the financial institution or card issuer to prevent a charge or request a reversal. Arranging the physical return is an operational step and does not authorize unjustified refusal or delay in recognizing the right of withdrawal.

### 9.4. Condition of a Product Returned Upon Withdrawal

We recommend keeping components, manuals, gifts, accessories, and, when available, the original packaging. Reasonable opening of the package and ordinary inspection of the product's characteristics do not automatically eliminate the right of withdrawal. Lack of the original packaging, by itself, is not automatic grounds for denying a legally valid return.

The consumer must allow the product to be returned and cooperate with the return logistics. Destruction, use beyond what is compatible with ordinary inspection, or deliberate absence of essential components will be assessed individually in light of the facts and the law, without an automatic penalty or blanket exclusion of the right.

## 10. Product Warranties and Technical Service

### 10.1. Statutory Warranty and Scope

Musical instruments, electronic equipment, and other durable goods are protected by law for claims involving apparent or readily identifiable defects for **90 days** from actual delivery. For non-durable products or services, when sold, the statutory period is **30 days**. For a hidden defect, the statutory claim period begins when the problem becomes apparent, subject to useful-life rules, liability, and other applicable provisions.

The statutory warranty applies regardless of a certificate, product registration with the manufacturer, or purchase of additional coverage. Proof of purchase may be established by a tax invoice, order, payment receipt, or other reliable means. The original packaging is not required to file a defect claim.

### 10.2. Additional Sonora Contractual Warranty

For **new guitars, basses, acoustic guitars, keyboards, audio interfaces, and microphones** sold directly by Sonora, an additional **9-month** contractual warranty may be offered on top of the 90-day statutory warranty period when the product is expressly marked with the **Proteção Sonora 9+** seal in the offer and in the certificate provided to the consumer. A contractual warranty is optional for other categories and cannot be presumed merely because the store's brand appears in the advertisement.

The certificate identifies the product, coverage, responsible service provider, procedures, terms, and service area. The 9-month contractual warranty begins after the relevant statutory period ends, without restricting rights concerning hidden defects or other protections provided by law. If the manufacturer provides broader or more favorable coverage, the corresponding documentation will be honored as offered.

### 10.3. Defects, Malfunctions, and Deterioration Through Use

The technical assessment distinguishes manufacturing defects, operating failures, failure to meet promised characteristics, normal wear, inadequate maintenance, and damage resulting from an external event. Strings, drumsticks, drumheads, picks, and connectors subject to wear may have varying service lives; this does not remove responsibility for manufacturing defects present when supplied.

Oxidation caused exclusively by improper exposure to water, accidental impacts, use with incompatible electrical voltage, technical modifications unrelated to the reported issue, and interventions proven to have caused the failure may not be covered by the contractual warranty. The statutory warranty is not automatically excluded merely because the customer used the equipment professionally, removed a nonessential label, or performed maintenance unrelated to the alleged defect.

### 10.4. How to Request Warranty Service

Customers may open a request through **Help > Warranty and repair**, identifying the product and, when available, the order, and describing the symptoms and the conditions in which they occur. Images, short videos, or accounts of safe tests may help but do not replace the relevant assessment and are not an absolute requirement.

Customer service will provide initial troubleshooting guidance and arrange referral to an authorized service provider, collection, or delivery to an appropriate location. Consumers must not open energized equipment, perform tests involving electrical hazards, or remove seals to comply with remote instructions. Transportation procedures will take the product's weight and fragility into account.

### 10.5. Time to Remedy a Defect

For cases governed by Article 18 of the Elarian Consumer Fairness Code, the supplier generally has up to **30 days** to remedy the defect, subject to legal situations in which the consumer may immediately exercise other remedies. The period will not be artificially restarted by repeated returns for the same defect or by simply referring the matter between suppliers or service providers.

If the defect is not remedied within the statutory period, the consumer may choose, as applicable, replacement with a product of the same kind in proper condition, immediate repayment of the amount paid with monetary adjustment, or a proportional price reduction, without prejudice to other rights. When the extent of the defect, replacement of parts, or the product's particular characteristics compromise its quality, features, or value, or when the product is essential, the legal alternatives may be available without waiting for the ordinary repair period, as provided by law.

For a defect for which the supplier is responsible, necessary diagnostic, repair, replacement, and logistics measures will not impose an improper cost on the consumer. If objective evidence shows that the problem resulted exclusively from a cause not covered by the contractual or statutory warranty, the company will provide a separate quote for any private service, which will be performed only with prior approval.

Prior use and previously described damage will be documented in the offer. Display, pre-owned, and refurbished products retain applicable statutory rights and may not be subject to a blanket exclusion of the statutory warranty. Changes in temperature, humidity, and transportation may require adjustments to tuning, the truss rod, string action, or intonation. ### 14.1. Purpose and Enrollment

**Harmonia+** is a free points and benefits program for registered consumers who optionally agree to its terms. Participation requires an individual account with valid identification; minors require legal representation or assistance when applicable. Enrollment is not required to purchase products, request warranty service, or exercise statutory rights.

Points are promotional benefits, not currency; they do not earn interest and may not be sold, directly converted into cash, or freely transferred between accounts. Benefits already granted and rights acquired under campaign rules will be honored.

### 14.2. Purchases That Earn Points

For every **$10.00** actually paid for eligible products, a participant earns **1 point**; fractions below $10.00 in the order's total eligible amount are disregarded. The calculation is based on the net subtotal of eligible products after discounts and before shipping. Services, shipping, gift cards, and refunded amounts do not earn points. Promotional items may earn points based on the price actually paid unless expressly and validly excluded by the campaign.

The calculation is based on all eligible products in an order, not on each unit separately. For example, an eligible net subtotal of **$289.90** earns **28 points**, provided the purchase is ultimately retained. Paying in installments does not change the number of points, provided payment is recognized.

### 14.3. When Points Are Credited

Points for online purchases are released **14 consecutive days after all eligible products have been delivered**, provided payment is confirmed and there is no cancellation or return that would invalidate the benefit. For orders with separate shipments, the system may release points for each delivered item if the amounts can be identified. In-store purchases follow the same release period of **14 consecutive days after purchase**, subject to later returns.

The commercial release period does not prevent consumers from reporting a crediting error. If there is a discrepancy, the team will verify eligible products, discounts applied, and the order's financial status.

### 14.4. Point Value and Redemption

Each **100 points** may be redeemed for **$10.00** off eligible products. The minimum redemption is 100 points, always in multiples of 100. For each order, redemption may not exceed **20% of the subtotal of products eligible for redemption** or their net price. No cash change is provided for a points balance insufficient for another redemption increment.

By default, products eligible for redemption are **regular-priced products**, excluding shipping, services, gift cards, and items expressly marked as ineligible. Redemption cannot be combined with a product discount coupon or the Sonora Transfer discount in Section 6.5. If an order includes both regular-priced and sale items, the benefit applies exclusively to eligible regular-priced items and does not affect the price of the others.

### 14.5. Checkout Calculation Order

Checkout first determines each product's price and discounts. It then calculates the program-eligible base and offers points redemption when compatible with the payment terms. The points discount is allocated among eligible regular-priced items in proportion to their prices, rounded to cents. The final subtotal is then calculated to check any free-shipping terms.

Before confirming the purchase, the customer can see the total points used, discount granted, shipping method, and final amount. The company may not charge extra afterward to correct a points application it accepted and validly recorded.

### 14.6. Validity and Expiration

Points expire **12 months after the date each batch is credited**. Upon redemption, points with the nearest expiration date are used first. The statement shows credits, redemptions, reversals, and expiration dates; customers can check their balance under **Harmonia+ > Statement**.

Advance expiration notices are an informational benefit, not a condition for point validity. Demonstrated system failures that prevent timely redemption will be reviewed for an appropriate restoration of the benefit.

### 14.7. Limits, Identification, and Misuse

Each person must use their own account and may not simulate purchases or create artificial returns to obtain benefits. The company may investigate suspicious transactions using proportionate criteria and retain records needed for the investigation. Blocking disputed points does not authorize suspension of legitimate orders without proper grounds or use of personal data in a manner incompatible with the applicable legal bases.

### 14.8. Cancellation, Returns, and Point Restoration

If a purchase that earned points is canceled or returned, the points corresponding to the amount not retained will be removed from the balance, including points already released. If points have already been used, the company may adjust future promotional balances without unilaterally deducting monetary amounts from a refund legally due.

When an order paid for using points is canceled in full, the points used will be restored after cancellation is processed, retaining their original expiration date. If that date passed while a statutory return was being processed, the restored points may be used for **30 consecutive days** after being returned to the balance. For a partial return, points will be restored in proportion to how they were originally applied to the returned items. Amounts paid in cash will be refunded separately under Section 12.

### 14.9. Changes to and Closure of the Program

The company may change future rules or close the program after clear advance notice through official channels, while respecting rights already acquired and terms of benefits granted. Points validly accumulated will not be retroactively removed through an arbitrary rule change. If the program closes, a reasonable redemption period or another appropriate solution for existing rights will be offered and announced in advance.

### 14.10. Relationship Between Loyalty and Customer Service

Participation or benefit level does not reduce the rights of non-participating consumers. The program may offer differentiated commercial benefits but does not change statutory periods for warranties, withdrawal, remedies, or responses to personal data requests.

## 15. Exceptional Situations and Contingency Procedures

### 15.1. Payment Confirmation Failure

If the consumer provides proof of payment but the order remains **Awaiting payment**, customer service will check the financial provider's records and verify the identifier, amount, payee, and time. A receipt alone does not necessarily establish settlement, but the company will not require a second payment before investigating the transaction.

If payment is identified after an order attempt has been automatically closed, the company will check whether the purchase can be reactivated under its original terms. If that is not possible for a legitimate reason, the company will explain the available alternatives and refund the amounts received. The risk of an internal reconciliation error will not be arbitrarily transferred to the consumer.

### 15.2. System Failure During Campaigns

In the event of overload, slow performance, store portal outage, or cart inconsistency, Sonora may temporarily pause new transactions to preserve integrity and security. Orders already placed remain subject to their recorded terms and the law. Payment and authorization records will be reconciled to prevent duplicate charges.

If no order confirmation exists, an image of an interrupted cart alone will be assessed together with the other evidence of contracting; it will not automatically be presumed that every checkout attempt constitutes a completed order. Conversely, a system failure will not be used to conceal a purchase that can be established by valid records.

### 15.3. Unavailability After Payment

If an item is found to be unavailable after it has been sold, the store must promptly inform the customer and present applicable legal alternatives. An inferior model will not be shipped without authorization, and store credit will not be imposed instead of a refund due. Waiting for restocking is an option only if the consumer agrees to a clear and reasonable timeline.

If the delay exceeds the contracted estimate without an accepted solution, the customer may exercise applicable rights for failure to fulfill the offer. The supplier may not set a new date based on the consumer's silence or assume that the customer accepted an indefinite delay.

### 15.4. Strikes, Road Closures, and Weather Events

Strikes, road closures, floods, accidents, traffic restrictions, and severe weather may affect pickups and deliveries. The company will seek alternative routes, locate shipments, and communicate promptly, providing a new follow-up milestone where possible. These events do not, by themselves, authorize a blanket refusal to refund or automatically remove consumer rights relating to the contract.

Customer service will assess the specific situation and alternatives for fulfillment, rescheduling with the customer's agreement, or termination. The customer will not be assigned uncontracted additional logistics costs solely because an external transportation event occurred.

### 15.5. Prolonged Customer Service Outage

If digital channels are unavailable, the company will publish a viable alternative in the store portal as soon as possible. Records made by the customer through verifiable channels during the outage will be considered when assessing statutory deadlines, preserving evidence of timely attempts. Operational outages do not authorize the suppression of withdrawal rights, defect claims, or data protection rights.

### 15.6. Manufacturing or Import Problems

Special orders dependent on suppliers may be affected by discontinuation, customs delays, or regulatory restrictions. The company will communicate material effects and seek contractually appropriate alternatives, including fulfillment where possible, an equivalent product with express acceptance, or a refund as required by law. A product originally contracted may not be unilaterally replaced with a different version merely because its technical characteristics are similar.

### 15.7. Recurrent Batch Issues and Recalls

If there are indications of a safety issue or recurring defect in a particular batch, Sonora may temporarily suspend sales, consult manufacturers and authorities, advise customers to stop using the product, and arrange corrective measures. Affected consumers will be notified through available means, subject to privacy rules and legal requirements for information and any corrective action or recall notice.

If there is a risk of electric shock, fire, detached parts, or another significant danger, harm prevention takes priority over routine commercial operations, without charging for measures that are the responsibility of the supply chain.

### 15.8. Incorrect Data and Suspected Unauthorized Access

Unexpected changes to an address, recipient, account, or history may indicate an error or fraud. The company may temporarily pause a high-risk action for verification, preserving the order and informing the account holder of possible next steps through a trusted channel. The block will be proportionate and reviewable and will not justify exposing sensitive information or requiring payment to remove it.

If there is evidence of unauthorized access or a data breach, the measures in Section 13.12 and appropriate security guidance will apply.

### 15.9. Unavailable Parts or Authorized Service

Difficulty obtaining a replacement part or finding an authorized service provider does not eliminate legal obligations to remedy a product defect. If repair is not feasible within the applicable period, the consumer may exercise the legally available options. The company will not extend the process indefinitely on the grounds that it is waiting for an import.

### 15.10. Discrepancy Between Tracking and Actual Receipt

A carrier's **Delivered** status is not conclusive proof that the recipient received the shipment. If disputed, the company will review proof of delivery, recipient identification, address, time, and other available evidence, respecting privacy. Customers will not be told to obtain personal data from neighbors or building staff as the sole condition for an investigation.

If it is determined that delivery was not properly completed, the company will take the appropriate steps to fulfill the offer or refund the purchase, without improperly charging for reshipment.

### 15.11. Disasters Affecting the Physical Store

In the event of a fire, flood, building closure, or other situation that prevents in-person service or pickup, the company will communicate alternatives for service, rescheduling, and delivery. Paid orders not yet delivered will be identified and handled individually. The event does not automatically require the consumer to travel to another city or impose additional costs without agreement.

### 15.12. Products with Shipping Restrictions

Products containing batteries, maintenance liquids, special materials, or components subject to transportation regulations may be offered only through permitted methods. The restriction must be disclosed in the item listing or during contracting. If the restriction is discovered after payment due to a store error, legally appropriate solutions will be offered, including a refund when due, without a penalty to the buyer.

### 15.13. Conflict Between Automated Service and This Policy

Responses from chatbots, virtual assistants, and automated systems are for guidance. In the event of a discrepancy, the team must consult the actual transaction, this policy, and statutory rights. An automated response cannot cancel a contractual term already accepted or restrict a consumer right.

If the assistant does not have reliable information—such as confirmation of a payment still pending, the complete contents of a tax invoice, a third party's authorization, or an inconclusive technical diagnosis—it must state the limitation and refer the request to an appropriate channel. It must not invent an order status, availability, refund timeline, or technical decision.

## 16. Applying the Rules to Combined Situations

### 16.1. Online Order with In-Store Pickup

Pickup does not automatically turn a purchase completed online into an in-store purchase. When the contract was entered into outside the commercial establishment, the right of withdrawal is governed by Section 9.2, subject to the applicable statutory starting points. Presenting the product at the store for pickup does not, by itself, remove the right applicable to the contracting method.

If the purchase was actually completed at the physical store, with the product selected and the contract made in person, the preference exchange benefit is governed by Sections 9.6 and 9.7. The mere existence of a later digital record does not create the distance-contract right of withdrawal.

### 16.2. Sale Product with a Defect

A promotional discount does not change the statutory warranty or transfer to the customer the cost of correcting a defect attributable to the supply. The company must assess the rights in Section 10 regardless of whether the product was a last unit, purchased with a coupon, or part of a campaign that has ended. A promotional restriction on voluntary exchanges does not override a defect claim or failure to fulfill the offer.

### 16.3. Customized Product Purchased Online

The fact that a product is customized determines manufacturing information, timing, and any commercial difficulty with resale; it does not, by itself, create a general exclusion from statutory withdrawal rights. Consumer rights are also preserved for a defect or a difference from the promised customization. The specific contract and the law will be considered, without automatic denial based solely on engraving, painting, or an individual adjustment.

### 16.4. Free Shipping and Partial Returns

If an order qualified for free economy shipping by exceeding a **$599.00** net subtotal and the customer returns only one item, the company will not retroactively charge shipping that was properly granted on the original order. The item's refund is based on the price actually paid for it, with proportional discounts taken into account. If all items are returned under a valid withdrawal, neither outbound nor return shipping will be charged.

If a partial return involves an incorrect or defective item, the store is responsible for the correction costs that fall to it, even if the order was shipped free of charge.

### 16.5. Order with a Coupon and Points Redemption

A product discount coupon and Harmonia+ points redemption are incompatible by default in the same order. Checkout must allow the customer to choose which benefit applies, without silently combining them. A campaign may expressly authorize stacking that one coupon with points. That authorization does not allow a second coupon on the order.

Points may be earned on eligible promotional products based on the amount actually paid, but **redemption** is restricted to regular-priced products and the terms in Section 14.4. The mere presence of a sale-priced product in an order does not prevent redemption on other eligible regular-priced items.

### 16.6. Sonora Transfer Discount, Points, and Free Shipping

The 3% Sonora Transfer discount, when available, applies only to eligible products and cannot be combined with points redemption or a product discount coupon. Free economy shipping may still be available if the order's net subtotal, after all discounts actually applied, reaches **$599.00** and the postal code and items qualify for that method.

For example, if the subtotal of eligible products is **$600.00** and all receive a 3% Sonora Transfer discount, the final subtotal is **$582.00**. In that case, the standard $599.00 free-shipping threshold is not met unless a specific campaign offers more favorable terms. The price and shipping cost must be shown before payment.

### 16.7. Cancellation After Dispatch but Before Delivery

Physical inability to intercept a package does not remove withdrawal or statutory termination rights. The company will provide appropriate instructions for refusing delivery, returning the package, or arranging collection, as applicable. No return shipping will be charged to the customer for a valid statutory withdrawal or a supply failure.

Changing the operational status to **Shipped** must not automatically result in denial of a legally available cancellation.

### 16.8. Defective Product After the Preference Exchange Period

The 15-day commercial period for voluntary exchanges does not limit statutory periods for defect claims. A durable item with an apparent defect during the statutory 90-day period, or a hidden defect for which the statutory period began when the problem appeared, must be assessed under Section 10 regardless of whether the preference exchange period has ended.

### 16.9. Delivery Delay and Price Change

An order with a delayed delivery remains subject to the price and terms agreed at contracting. The consumer does not have to pay a difference resulting from a later price increase. A later price reduction does not automatically revise the order, but it does not change rights relating to the delay or failure to fulfill the offer.

### 16.10. Partially Delivered Order and Withdrawal

When separate items are delivered on different days, the practical withdrawal period for each physical item purchased at a distance is calculated from its receipt. Delivery of one item does not start the period for another item that has not yet been delivered. If the items form an indivisible set contracted as such, the assessment considers complete delivery and applicable statutory rights.

### 16.11. Purchase by a Business Entity

Sales to business entities will be assessed according to the actual legal classification of the consumer relationship. Issuing a tax invoice in a business entity's name does not, by itself, constitute a waiver of protections under the Elarian Consumer Fairness Code when a consumer relationship exists. Commercial terms for wholesale, professional resale, or business contracting will be stated before contracting and will not override mandatory rules that apply to the particular case.

### 16.12. Unclear Rule or Unaddressed Case

If this document does not contain enough information to determine an amount, deadline, eligibility, or order status, the customer must receive a transparent explanation of what is missing and how to confirm it. The company does not assume authorization to grant, deny, or promise a benefit based on unverified data. Unaddressed cases will be assessed under the contract, offer, law, and principles of good faith, with the reasons for the solution recorded.

## 17. Final Commitments to Consumers

### 17.1. Clear and Verifiable Information

Sonora commits to providing essential information about products, prices, availability, delivery timelines, special terms, and additional services before purchase. Consumers may review the order summary before paying and later access order information through official channels.

### 17.2. Protection of Statutory Rights

Voluntary exchange, loyalty, campaign, and additional-service terms supplement but do not replace rights under the Elarian Consumer Fairness Code. Terms that improperly reduce the supplier's liability, require waiver of non-waivable rights, or transfer prohibited risks will not be applied.

### 17.3. Reasoned Responses and Review

Support requests and complaints will be assessed according to their nature, without automatically denying a claim because of missing packaging, expiration of a promotional benefit or voluntary exchange period, or a generic reference to manufacturer rules. If there is an interpretive disagreement, the company will provide an objective explanation and available review channels.

### 17.4. Continuing the Relationship

Sonora seeks to maintain relationships of trust with musicians, students, audio professionals, and other customers by providing product-appropriate guidance, respectful service, and proportionate procedures. Each request must be resolved based on recorded facts, the terms actually agreed, and applicable law, without compromising safety, privacy, or consumer dignity.
